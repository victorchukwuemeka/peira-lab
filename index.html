A research laboratory that ships products.

We take the problems industries quietly run on — payments, inventory, networks, privacy — and ship working systems to the people who live with them every day. Not decks, not prototypes. Products.

There is no house style. Where the problem has no signal, we build offline. Where the problem is trust, we build in protocols. Where the problem is physics, we build hardware. Every build is ported to what the problem and the solution demand.

Research

The method, in four steps

01

Find the real problem. The boring, repeated, money-or-freedom-losing step an entire industry runs on.

02

Study it on the ground. We walk the counter, the network, the field. We do not guess from a questionnaire.

03

Port to the problem. The whole system is shaped by what the problem actually demands — not by a house style.

04

Ship and maintain. Compile it, run it, keep it alive. A product that is not maintained stops working.

Mission

One question follows every project in the lab — what happens from the moment this enters, until money comes out?

It is asked of a dry cleaner's counter and a devnet's shard stream alike. Nigeria is home to roughly 40 million businesses; fewer than 5% use any kind of modern software. Most run on notebooks, WhatsApp and memory. Half the lab studies how those businesses work, and ships the tools for them. The other half studies infrastructure — networks, operating systems, hardware, protocols — so the tools never depend on a technology we are not willing to build.

Same question everywhere: what happens from the moment this enters, until money comes out?
